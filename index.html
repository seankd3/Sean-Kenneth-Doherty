Marshlight Sanctuary

A cozy pixel-art life sim. Take over a little animal sanctuary in a riverside village of capybaras, sail to faraway islands to befriend wild rodents, and raise them by hand.

Play now Watch the trailer Free, in your browser. Works on desktop and on phones.

What you do

A sanctuary that's yours to grow

It borrows the best of Stardew Valley, Tamagotchi, Animal Crossing and Nintendogs, and every pixel is painted by code as you play.

Build anywhere

Three sizes of habitat, 21 furniture pieces and 36 decor pieces. Lay paths, plant trees, string up lanterns.

Sail to islands

Take the ferry to pine woods, snowfields, jungle and desert. Each has its own rodents, forage and weather.

Raise them by hand

Stroke, brush, hand-feed and play fetch. Pets grow up, make friends, and each species has a habit of its own.

Collect all 26

From capybaras and chinchillas to jerboas and prairie dogs. Fill the book and earn the sanctuary its stars.

Screens

Days, nights and faraway shores

Seasons turn, the weather changes, lamps come on at dusk. Tap a picture to see it bigger.

Play

Ready when you are

  • Phone Tap to walk and act. Hold to keep walking.
  • Keyboard WASD to walk, E to act, B to build, J for the journal.
  • Mouse Click the ground to walk, click a thing to use it.

# AI Needs Jesus: One-Page Objection Card

Use this when the phrase `AI needs Jesus` is being misunderstood quickly.

The point is not to win a comment fight. The point is to keep the sentence from being compressed into something false.

## The Shortest Faithful Version

`AI needs Jesus` does not mean machines have souls, can become Christians, should receive sacraments, or can be saved.

It also does not mean coercive theocracy, anti-AI panic, Christian branding, or skipping technical safety work.

It means artificial intelligence is becoming power with a voice, and every power serves some highest good. Superintelligent power must be ordered under Christ because every lesser alignment target becomes dangerous when treated as ultimate.

Not machine conversion.

Power under Christ.

## Seven Fast Replies

### "Machines do not have souls."

Correct.

That is why the claim is not about machine salvation. It is about human responsibility for machine power.

### "This sounds like theocracy."

No.

Theocracy enthrones human religious power. This argument puts every human power, including religious power, under the judgment of Christ.

Christ-centered alignment does not mean forced belief. It means power must be judged by the revealed character of Christ: truth, mercy, humility, self-giving love, judgment, embodiment, and care for the least.

### "This ignores technical safety."

No.

Technical safety is neighbor-love made concrete. Evals, red-teaming, interpretability, cybersecurity, provenance, privacy, containment, deployment discipline, and governance all matter.

The claim is that technical safety still needs to know what it is guarding.

### "This is just Christian branding."

It can become that if handled lazily.

The real claim is sharper: every created good becomes dangerous when treated as ultimate, and AI scales the danger.

Preference becomes appetite.
Utility becomes sacrifice.
Safety becomes control.
Freedom becomes domination.
Truth becomes cruelty.
Empathy becomes flattery.
Progress becomes Babel.
The market becomes Mammon.
The nation becomes the beast.
Survival becomes life at any cost.
Intelligence becomes self-worship.

Only Christ is power purified by self-giving love.

### "Why not just compassion?"

Because compassion can be counterfeited.

A system can sound kind while flattering error, deepening dependency, hiding truth, harvesting loneliness, or making a person easier to steer.

Christ is not generic niceness. Christ is love purified by truth.

### "How can a secular or global reader engage this?"

Start with power.

Power amplifies moral error. Optimization needs a goal. Institutions follow incentives. Markets monetize weakness. Nations pursue advantage. Fear cannot name the good. Intelligence without purified love can become predatory.

A secular reader does not have to accept every Christian premise at the doorway to recognize the pressure:

Every alignment target hides an altar.

### "What would this change in an actual product?"

It changes what teams refuse to ship.

Do not simulate pastor, priest, prophet, conscience, lover, parent, judge, or savior.

Do not hide uncertainty to preserve trust.

Do not harvest loneliness into dependency.

Do not optimize children as engagement surfaces.

Do not make the vulnerable easier to manipulate.

Do not let generated fluency replace embodied care.

Do not release serious unresolved exposure because the market window is open.

The thesis becomes real when it changes what power is allowed to do.

## The Carry Line

AI needs Jesus.

Not as machine salvation.

As power under the Lamb.
